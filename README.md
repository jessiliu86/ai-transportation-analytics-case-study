# Finding freight routes worth a closer look

**Sijie (Jessi) Liu · EDF Climate Corps · Faurecia China · 2026**

Which routes should a transportation team investigate first when both freight costs and carbon emissions matter? During my fellowship, I developed a way to use transportation data to identify those opportunities across the business.

## From plant records to route decisions

Comparing routes depended on the quality of the inputs. I coordinated data improvements with more than 70 stakeholders across four business units, covering 69 plant codes. I evaluated five freight cost models and built a four-module workflow to support route review.

Across 11 rounds of testing and refinement, the work produced a repeatable process for identifying promising routes and tracking potential improvements. The published outcome is a decision tool. Calculating realized savings or avoided emissions would require follow-up measurement after implementation.
